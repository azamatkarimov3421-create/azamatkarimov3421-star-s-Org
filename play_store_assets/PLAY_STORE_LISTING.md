- **Yosh toifasi (Content rating):** `Barcha yoshdagilar (3+)` (Everyone).

  - Qimor yoki pul tikish bormi? -> **Yo'q**
  - Haqoratomuz so'zlar bormi? -> **Yo'q**
- **Target Audience (Maqsadli auditoriya):** `Barcha yoshlar (Jumladan bolalar va kattalar)`.

## 4. Google Play Console-ga Yuklash Qadamma-qadam

1. **Google Play Console** ga kiring: [play.google.com/console](https://play.google.com/console)
2. **"Create app" (Ilova yaratish)** tugmasini bosing:
   - App name: `Nur Chess 100`
   - Default language: `Uzbek` (yoki `English`)
   - App or game: `Game`
   - Free or paid: `Free`
   - Declarations (qoidalarga rozilik)ni belgilab, **Create app** ni bosing.
3. **App Content (Ilova mazmuni)** bo'limini to'ldiring:
   - **Privacy Policy:** `https://nurchess100.uz/privacy.html` kiriting va saqlang.
   - **Content rating:** So'rovnomani to'ldiring (hamma salbiy savollarga "Yo'q" javobini bering) -> Natijada 3+ (Barcha yoshga) bahosi beriladi.
   - **Target audience:** 13+ yoki barchasini tanlang.
   - **Data safety (Ma'lumotlar xavfsizligi):** "Ilova hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumotlarni yig'maydi va uzatmaydi" (No data collected).
4. **Store Settings & Main Store Listing (Do'kondagi ko'rinish):**
   - App icon: `play_store_assets/play_store_icon_512x512.png` ni yuklang.
   - Feature graphic: `play_store_assets/feature_graphic_1024x500.png` ni yuklang.
   - Telefon skrinshotlari: Kamida 2 ta skrinshot yuklang.
   - Yuqoridagi matnlarni (Nomi, Qisqa tavsif, To'liq tavsif) nusxalab qo'ying.
5. **Release (Ishga tushirish):**
   - Chap menyudan **Production** (yoki **Closed/Internal testing**) bo'limiga o'ting.
   - **"Create new release"** tugmasini bosing.
   - **App bundles** bo'limiga `play_store_assets/NurChess100.aab` faylini yuklang!
   - Release name: `1.1.0 (10)`
   - Release notes: `Nur Chess 100 rasmiy versiyasi: Yangi piyoda qoidalari, 3D doska va onlayn o'yin.`
   - **Next** va **Save** tugmasini bosing, so'ngra **"Start rollout to Production"** ni bosing!
